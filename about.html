Since 2008

Colourful, fun, made for the island.

Island Haze launched in 2008 for a young audience, with a colourful and fun style. It is an urban clothing brand for teenagers, trend-setters and the young at heart, offering quality, affordable and edgy streetwear in Mauritius.

What we make

T-shirts with a message, floral dresses and casual streetwear, from beach dresses and boho-chic tops to classic polos, swimwear and jackets.

Where to find us

Riche Terre Mall, Cœur de Ville Flacq, Cascavelle Mall and La Croisette in Grand Baie, or online at islandhaze.mu.
